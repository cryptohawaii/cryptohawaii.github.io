What Are Cryptocurrencies?

Cryptocurrencies are digital, decentralized forms of money that use blockchain technology for secure transactions. Popular examples include Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), and Ripple (XRP). They solve the "double-spending" problem without needing a central authority, relying on mathematical consensus mechanisms like proof-of-work or proof-of-stake. As of 2026, cryptocurrencies continue to evolve, offering innovations in finance, NFTs, and decentralized apps (dApps).

How to Buy Cryptocurrency in Hawaii

As of July 1, 2024, cryptocurrency is fully legal to buy, sell, and trade in Hawaii. Virtual asset service providers no longer require a state-issued money transmitter license, making it easier for residents to access crypto platforms. This change followed the conclusion of the Digital Currency Innovation Lab (DCIL) pilot program, which ran from 2020 to 2022 and confirmed that such licenses are unnecessary for digital asset activities.

Hawaii residents can use popular exchanges like Coinbase, Binance.US, or Kraken to purchase crypto securely. Always prioritize licensed platforms and enable two-factor authentication for safety.
