Effective date: June 18, 2026

Hawaii Crypto Currency publishes educational articles about cryptocurrency, blockchain, digital assets, consumer safety, and financial technology as they relate to Hawaii residents, families, small businesses, and local policy.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make digital finance easier to understand without promoting speculation. We prioritize local relevance, clear definitions, source links, scam-prevention guidance, and practical context for readers who may be new to blockchain or fintech.

What We Cover

  • Hawaii cryptocurrency and fintech regulation.
  • Consumer protection, fraud prevention, and crypto scam awareness.
  • Beginner explanations of wallets, exchanges, blockchains, stablecoins, and digital payments.
  • Local economic context, including remittances, tourism, small-business payments, and public-sector technology.
  • National or global crypto developments when they have a clear connection to Hawaii readers.

Sourcing Standards

For laws, agency actions, bills, market claims, public officials, company claims, and statistics, we try to rely on primary or clearly attributable sources such as state records, agency publications, court filings, official company announcements, and established news outlets. When a fact can change quickly, we use concrete dates and avoid presenting old information as current.

No Personal Financial Advice

Articles are for general education only. We do not provide personal financial, investment, legal, tax, or accounting advice. We do not tell readers to buy, sell, trade, stake, hold, or use any cryptocurrency, token, exchange, wallet, protocol, or financial product.

Corrections

If we find a material error, we update the article and, when appropriate, add a note explaining what changed. Readers can report possible corrections through the contact page.

Advertising And Independence

This site may display advertising. Ads, affiliate relationships, sponsored placements, or third-party links are not editorial endorsements. Sponsored or paid content, if published, should be clearly labeled.

AI Assistance

We may use software tools, including AI-assisted drafting or editing tools, to organize research, improve clarity, or prepare images. Editorial responsibility remains with the publisher. Articles should be reviewed for factual accuracy, local relevance, and compliance with this policy before publication.
