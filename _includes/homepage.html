Foundations · Education

What Are Cryptocurrencies?

Cryptocurrencies are digital, decentralized forms of money secured by cryptographic hashing and public blockchains rather than a central bank. Major networks—including Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), Solana (SOL), and XRP—use Proof-of-Work or Proof-of-Stake consensus to prevent double-spending and settle transactions globally in minutes.

Post-DCIL Access · Resident Guide

How to Buy Cryptocurrency in Hawaii

Since July 1, 2024—following the conclusion of Hawaiʻi's Digital Currency Innovation Lab (DCIL)—virtual currency companies no longer require a state-issued money transmitter license, opening access to major U.S.-regulated exchanges while federal FinCEN and tax rules remain in effect.

Compare platforms accessible in the islands, understand ACH vs. kiosk fees, and follow our security checklist before making a purchase.
