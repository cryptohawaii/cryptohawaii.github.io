Effective date: June 5, 2026

Hawaii Digital Currency, also known as Crypto Hawaii, provides educational content about cryptocurrency, blockchain, digital assets, and related financial technology topics for readers in Hawaii. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, such as your email address and message when you contact us through our Formspree-powered contact form. We may also collect basic technical information when you visit the site, including pages viewed, browser type, device information, referring pages, approximate location, and similar analytics data.

Analytics, Cookies, And Advertising

We use analytics tools, including Google Analytics, to understand site traffic and improve our crypto education content. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits, popular pages, and general usage patterns.

We may display advertising through Google AdSense or other advertising partners. Advertising partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads, measure ad performance, and help prevent fraud. Google may use advertising cookies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this and other websites.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. You can also learn more about Google's advertising settings and choices through Google's published privacy and ads controls.

How We Use Information

We use collected information to operate the site, respond to messages, improve articles and educational resources, understand audience needs in Hawaii, maintain site security, and support advertising or analytics functions.

Formspree Contact Forms

Messages submitted through our contact form are processed by Formspree. Do not send private wallet keys, seed phrases, passwords, account credentials, tax records, or other highly sensitive information through the form. Contact form submissions are used only to review and respond to your inquiry.

Crypto Education Notice

Our content is for general education and informational purposes. We do not provide personalized financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Cryptocurrency and digital asset topics can involve significant risk, changing regulation, scams, volatility, and technical complexity. Readers should do their own research and consult qualified professionals for personal decisions.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal contact messages. We may share limited information with service providers that help operate this site, including hosting, analytics, advertising, spam prevention, and Formspree contact form processing. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the site, users, or public safety.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the contact page or email digitalcurrencyhawaii@gmail.com.
